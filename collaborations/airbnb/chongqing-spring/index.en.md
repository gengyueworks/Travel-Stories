# Chongqing · We Will All Reach the Shore, Bathed in Sunlight, Surrounded by Blooming Flowers

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

![Chongqing](images/002.jpg)

Hello, everyone. It's Yue.

I've just returned to Beijing, still glowing with the feeling of **"I really love Chongqing"** — the joy of falling in love with a city.

A most recent spontaneous trip. One Airbnb, and **my family and I truly fell in love with Chongqing. We fell in love with the idea of traveling during Chinese New Year**, and from now on I plan to set out every year, always eager for the next.

This trip was mainly to give my mom a change of scenery.

![Chongqing](images/003.jpg)

This was my private hope — I didn't tell her. Because my grandmother had just passed away, and a quiet, unspoken fragility and sorrow still lingered in the house.

When you travel with family, the words you dread hearing are "this is boring" or "not great at all."

But **my mom said "this is so lovely" many, many times.**

![Chongqing](images/004.jpg)

As I write this story, spring has arrived.

"Spring belongs to everyone. If the flowers have determination, if spring has determination, then we can have the same determination."

A gift of spring.

Today, let me take you into a house surrounded by flowers.

**Setting Out is Always a Little Hard**

*· JUSTGO*

**"Going out and seeing things is so much more interesting than staying at home."**

My mom said this casually. During these days of travel, she laughed heartily every single time.

![My mom](images/023.jpg)

But our conversations before departure were nothing like that.

My mom said: *It's cold. The luggage is heavy. Don't run around during the New Year. Flights are expensive. You go play by yourself. I don't want to go.*

I said: *Don't you have a plan to travel to many cities across China? That's how you make it happen — one city at a time.*

*If every time you think it's too much trouble and let it go, you'll never set out, and in the end you'll go nowhere. Right?*

In the end, we went together. JUSTGO.

![Chongqing](images/024.jpg)

**"Beauty is everywhere, but you often need to walk into a garden to discover it." More importantly, my family and I fell in love with this way of "reuniting in a faraway place."**

Seeing a reader's New Year comment saying they were "avoiding the holidays," I want to share some heartfelt words too.

I've actually never really liked holidays. For many years, my New Year's Days and Spring Festivals were completely drowned in a mountain of work. The wish of traveling with family seemed like a delusion.

I remember a Weibo post I liked during a late night years ago:

"When you toil in suffering, your tears are mixed with blood. They flow freely, crossing glaciers. That is also a kind of greatness — a ruthless greatness. It can break ice, it can split mountains… But you wouldn't know: **blood and tears flowing through glaciers will eventually meet warm oceans, where there are grasslands, clear springs, the fragrance of flowers, and the other shore.**" (From Nie Xinyuan)

In those days of standing in bone-chillingly cold rivers, we were also living earnestly. "By a determination that is bound to be realized."

Flowers are a kind of fate, and also a beautiful symbol. I give them to you too.

We will all reach the shore. Bathed in sunlight for miles. Wherever we go, flowers will bloom.

![Flowers](images/025.jpg)

I had even always been afraid of vacationing in Chinese cities during holidays. After this trip, I'm not so afraid anymore.

My mom was even more excited than I was, talking with me about travel plans to different cities across China, full of anticipation and engagement.

"This world — there is still something worth loving.

After this Spring Festival trip with family, it feels like we love this world even more.

**This passionate and gentle world, which we get to have only once. Create more beautiful moments, so the feeling of love never breaks.**"
