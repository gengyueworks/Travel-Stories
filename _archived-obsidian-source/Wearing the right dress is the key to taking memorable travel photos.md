# [Wearing the right dress is the key to taking memorable travel photos](https://carrot-apricot-kdwk.squarespace.com/blog/2018/9/25/wearing-the-right-dress-is-the-key-to-taking-memorable-travel-photos)

**May we all become “girls on the road” who will dress.** Maybe on you, innocent and mature, handsome and sexy, retro and modern can exist at the same time, and meet to create a stunning self.

Wearing a right pair of outfits is the key to taking memorable travel photos. **I will restore the preparation process and matching principle before departure. How to use it.**

Before each trip, preparing clothes is **the most enjoyable but also the most enjoyable part**. Everyone can create a brand-new, blooming self-according to their own personality and preferences, combined with local landscape features.

[GREECE](https://carrot-apricot-kdwk.squarespace.com/)

“following the sense of inspiration”

Keywords: favourite blue & Prepare carefully

Greece has used up the best **blue** in the world.

This is my first time on a trip, consciously preparing clothes. **Because it is Greece**, I have finally arrived in the land of dreams.


![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/0*3j_dAbV8YTxmMJto.jpeg)

**How to prepare**

Time: The week before the start of the journey.

Restore the preparation process at the time:

- Open the itinerary for Greece and bring up the name and keywords of the destination, Day 1 Day 2…going to go every day.
- Put the keywords into the **Google Images/Maps search** and you will see the island/city directly.
- After seeing the photo, I will choose the appropriate colour and match the clothes.
- I think about a few days, I need a few sets of look-ups, and then start searching for filters on popular shopping sites/apps.

(This process is actually a little tedious, and you need to communicate with the merchant to confirm the size/arrival time, etc. It is recommended to prepare as early as possible.)

Here, blue and white clothes and blue sea and white houses are a perfect match.

## AFRICA, KENYA

“following the sense of inspiration”

[](https://medium.com/write?source=promotion_paragraph---post_body_banner_better_place_blocks--35e35d426edf---------------------------------------)

Keywords: denim short jacket + wiped shoulder dye long dress

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/0*34fUuixpfE_lE-YH.jpg)

**With regard to freedom and wildness in Kenya**, countless people have listed this as the place to visit in this life.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/0*6lvwxGuWDLG_f5FV.jpg)

Everyone wants to go to Africa, who has never been involved, with a desire to explore the unknown. In those few days, **I saw the beautiful sunrise of the Kilimanjaro snow mountain in the early morning, the dazzling starry sky and the minimalist beauty of the Maasai Mara grassland** — pure blue sky, vast, rolling grassland, and a single acacia tree growing alone.

This time I didn’t buy new clothes, **but matched some clothes that had been idle in the closet**.


![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/0*S88IUAKQLTplHW8H.jpg)

I have a long bohemian print dress, which conveys a slightly vagrant temperament when worn on its own.

But this time I went to Kenya I was a little tired of the same holiday dress. I wanted something different. At the time, I added **a short washed denim jacket,** which I had bought many years ago. In fact, I also had the opportunity to wear it. When these two items are put together, they demonstrate **a handsome and wild taste.**


![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/0*1_7_r-i7gtD981Ig.jpg)

And in that afternoon, my impression of Africa was completely subverted. The leaves covered the sky and the pink flowers bloomed. I turned a circle and thought of San Mao’s sentence: “The wind is in the forest, the bird is calling, and the dream is spent.” San Mao was also known as Echo or Echo Chan in English.

> My suggestion: pick some **old clothes** that you have already bought, according to the destination, and create a permutation and combination, a collision. There will be a surprise; maybe you usually do not wear the clothes at the bottom of the box, but very mirrored. **You can try them boldly and there are no taboos.** Just look good.
