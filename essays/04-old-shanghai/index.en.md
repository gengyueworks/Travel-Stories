### The 5th Hour in Shanghai

*Rongda/Rhonda's Airbnb*

Turning into the old alley of Shikumen, I walked into the fireworks from the bustling city. The old man was chatting on the small bench, and the cat and the puppy were playing hide and seek. The shoe repairer was eighty-six years old. He also wore a cap and reading glasses, sat in the mouth of the alley, and added heels to the shoes… People are real, living in peace.

![Alley entrance](images/005.jpg)

![Old man](images/006.jpg)

Here you can smell the taste of Shanghai people living. The afternoon sun was sprinkled on the windowsill, and the bamboo poles were covered with white shirts and quilts. The tailors cut the pants downstairs, and the shaver fixed the hair of the old man in the hall… In the ordinary trivia, in the alley, in the sound, I met the real and pure Shanghai.

"The taste buds and memories of people are lingering. When I can eat Shanghai cuisine like Rongda, I may also be able to perceive her love of youth."

### The 37th Hour in Shanghai

*Daren Gathering in the Alley*

This authentic Shanghai girl likes to make friends, and people send love called "a beautiful flower in Hongkou". In the home of her alley, there is a magical magnetic field. There are often more than a dozen young people gathered here to share their life stories. There are local architects who have come to Shanghai for its wild history. "I followed my father from a young age and walked through Shanghai step by step. Every road, I remember a lot of old road names." There are also female geeks from Silicon Valley, California. ### The 58th Hour in Shanghai

*Drifting on the Huangpu River Watching the Sunset*

In the setting sun, we spent two yuan taking a ferry across the Huangpu River to watch the "very bleak Shanghai sunset".

Drifting in the middle of the river, close your eyes and take a deep breath, lazy river wind, faint clouds and flowing flash of water. Opposite the Bund, the bell rings, and the dense buildings, the car exhaust fumes on the road and the depressing atmosphere on the pavement hundreds of metres away are completely pushed away by the river ferry.

![Huangpu River sunset](images/017.jpg)

For a moment, the magic in everyone's eyes seems to have become a peaceful, slow-paced city. The downtown area seems to be in the peaceful peach blossom source, the evening is悠闲 and the people are still quiet.
