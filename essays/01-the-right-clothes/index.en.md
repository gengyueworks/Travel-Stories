# Wearing the Right Clothes: The Secret to Stunning Travel Photos

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

**Here it is — the travel styling guide I owe you, straight from the heart.**

Every time I share photos from my trips, I get a flood of compliments on my outfits. 👉 *"I love how you insist on looking beautiful in photos no matter where you are — it creates such a wonderful picture."*

In my daily life, I don't really overdress. It's common for me to stumble out the door with messy hair and no makeup. But when I'm on the road, I actually think about what to wear each day — and it all happens naturally.

Every journey is a precious memory, like a little film. **You get to create the look, so you don't let the beauty of faraway places go to waste.**

I've sorted through the clothes and dresses I wore across different cities — ten cities, ten looks. Here's **Vol. 1.**

**I'll share the colors, inspirations, and moods behind each outfit. All free-form, but they'll definitely catch your eye in the photos.**

Wear a dress that suits you,

and set off on a beautiful journey around the world.

I lived in Shanghai for a month, and in the last few days there, I put together this denim jacket and pink skirt. **It was my good friend Zoey in Shanghai** who helped me stumble upon this pairing in just twenty minutes — a spark of brilliance.

The clothes are from the Indian national-treasure designer MANISH ARORA. Every piece in this brand's collection is handmade by experienced Indian artisans. The exotic yet modern designs carry the distinctive colors of traditional Indian culture — pink and gold.

For the designer, **fashion is not just clothing; it's a medium for conveying happiness.** I wore it to explore some of Shanghai's coolest buildings and spent a long time playing in front of the graffiti walls at M50.

![Inside a Shanghai building](images/010.jpg)

![Shanghai outfit close-up](images/011.jpg)

The effect of this outfit was incredible. For those two days, almost every stranger who passed by would stare at the clothes. Some lifted their phones to take photos, and a few even asked for a picture with me. When I posted a couple of shots to my feed, beauty-loving girls asked, "Where can I get this?"

This set was the only one in the country — DFO had just brought it back from Paris Fashion Week. A rare stroke of luck.

**Tip: On the road, be bold with colors you wouldn't normally dare to wear** — like this vivid pink. I was a little hesitant before putting it on, but it turned out to be incredibly eye-catching in photos.

**I've realized that in daily life I play it safe, but on the road, there's no color I won't try.**

### Morocco

![Jardin Majorelle, Marrakech](images/012.jpg)

![The Majorelle Blue](images/013.jpg)

![A corner of the garden](images/014.jpg)

In Marrakech, my favorite garden is **Jardin Majorelle** — a pure, sweeping blue that people call "Majorelle Blue."

The garden was designed by the illustrator Jacques Majorelle, who poured his life's passion into it. It later became the private garden of **Yves Saint Laurent.** Inside, you'll find YSL stories and works — you'll fall in love with the designer all over again.

![The garden's bright yellows and greens](images/015.jpg)

There are large swaths of bright yellow and lush green plants. **You can wear vivid colors to echo the backdrop.**

### The Inspiration

**Keyword: color clash, ethnic style**

![Moroccan ethnic-style color-clash outfit](images/016.jpg)

Because the architecture itself is so colorful, the clothes I brought to Morocco were all about **vivid ethnic style** — a perfect match for the exotic atmosphere. The colors are bright because this place is filled with fairy-tale hues. **You can clash colors freely, and it'll look great no matter what.**

If flashy clothes aren't your thing, you can also **bring clean, simple, elegant pieces and follow the subtraction principle.** Standing in the middle of all that color, it makes for beautiful photos too.

![Blue tie-dye dress](images/017.jpg)

![Pushing open a blue wooden door](images/018.jpg)

![A starry-sky-like curtain overhead](images/019.jpg)

This is a **blue tie-dye dress** that I absolutely love. In this blue-and-green-toned house in Morocco, I walked past the pool, pushed open a blue wooden door, and looked up at a canopy like the starry sky.

![Moroccan sheepskin slippers](images/020.jpg)

In the evening, we went to the local market in Marrakech with our host and bought **a pair of soft Moroccan sheepskin slippers** — incredibly comfortable, I could walk in them all day. I highly recommend wearing light, comfortable clothing and shoes when walking through Morocco in 40-degree heat.

Standing on the floor offers another kind of scenery — every shot is Instagram-worthy.

The sunset glow of Florence is rose-colored.

When you immerse yourself in this city of profound history and artistic heritage, it feels like an endless Renaissance-themed gallery and museum, steeped in a romantic and classical atmosphere.

*"Good taste is a necessity of life. Whether it's food, art, music, or fashion — beauty in all its forms moves me."*

### The Inspiration

**Keyword: purple dress + black jacket**

![Purple dress with a 360° skirt](images/024.jpg)

This dress has a 360° flared skirt — so fluid and graceful. The color is wisteria purple, and it conveys an elegant, enchanting feeling.

If your everyday style is more understated and casual, **bring a slightly dramatic long dress on your travels — it can elevate your entire presence.**

If you're tall and slim, wearing it highlights a graceful silhouette. **When taking photos, spin around in the dress.** The chiffon fabric moves unhurriedly — sometimes soaring high, sometimes skimming low, like a passing breeze.

![Spinning in the dress](images/025.jpg)

![Backless detail in a vintage hotel](images/026.jpg)

I wore it in a hundred-year-old vintage hotel in Florence, sitting on a retro black sofa. I chose to capture the "backless" detail — the design is so distinctive, and it creates a unique collision with the weight of history here.

Purple is rarely worn during travel, but this color can be styled in surprisingly refined ways.

### A Kind of Magical Styling Instinct

I've discovered that I naturally have a magical ability — a fashion sense. 👇

For example, I might suddenly learn I need to fly to a certain country with only **two days** left to prepare. I can buy **ten dresses** in **two hours**, wait **one day** for express delivery, and **twenty minutes** after receiving them, pack them straight into my suitcase and head to the airport.

Once I arrive, I look at the day's scenery and spend **fifteen minutes** in the morning picking an outfit to wear out.

As a photographer and content creator, I sometimes spend the whole day working with little opportunity to take photos of myself. Almost every shot of me was captured in just **a few minutes** — or sometimes even **ten seconds** — by asking a passerby or travel companion to press the shutter. **And that's how the photos you've seen came to be.**

![A candid moment on the road](images/027.jpg)

Every time I pack my clothes, the speed, the efficiency, the precision of my eye — even I surprise myself a little. **Today, I'm sharing some of the common threads behind this skill.**
