# 72 Hours in Search of Phuket's True Flavors

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

**Your next long weekend could be Phuket.**

Everyone says Phuket is the ideal place for ocean views and diving. **But there may be more here than just scenery.**

![Phuket beach](images/002.jpg)

There's the taste of nature here. The taste of the sea, the taste of the sun — **and the taste of love, the taste of human warmth.**

These flavors hide in the secret street food stalls, in the fresh ingredients at the market, mingling with the locals' warm, joyful nature. **They land on the tongue and then rise to the heart,** making it almost impossible to tell which is taste and which is feeling.

Three days, seventy-two hours. Through chance encounters and discoveries with Angie, I unlocked a Phuket you won't find in any guidebook.

**Hour 3 in Phuket**

***Angie's Airbnb***

Angie stood before the white Villa Alia, pushed open the door, and the blue swimming pool blazed under the fierce sun.

She couldn't wait to share what she loves. "**Life in Phuket is in color. Every day we can experience so many different kinds of beauty.** I love every moment here, honestly: the food, the beach, the sunshine… Several times a day, a voice inside me says, wow, this is amazing!"

![Villa Alia's pool](images/003.jpg)

The villa is named after her youngest daughter, Alia — her greatest love. "This house is like me. Finding it, buying it, was an enormous surprise." Angie loves beautiful things, artistic design, clean lines, and she has collected decor from all over the world to fill the rooms, piece by piece. "I just got back from Bali and bought some beautiful bamboo decorations. This home will become just a little bit lovelier."

![Villa Alia interior](images/004.jpg)

Four years ago, she made a willful decision: she gave up everything from over thirty years of a comfortable life in Belgium and moved the entire family to Phuket, Thailand. "Nobody understood. But I was just — so happy!"

![Angie](images/005.jpg)

"**Phuket is a dream. The life I'm living now is the life I used to dream of.** If I ever leave here, I'll have lost a part of my heart."

**Hour 10 in Phuket**

***Songkran Frenzy***

![Songkran celebration](images/006.jpg)

I spent the Thai New Year — Songkran — with the locals. **It was a day of such pure joy.**

![Songkran water fight](images/007.jpg)

![Songkran water fight](images/008.jpg)

"Water represents luck. It washes away the exhaustion and bad things of the past year. Once you experience it, you'll fall in love with it." Angie's whole family pulled me into a local celebration. Bucket after bucket of water came at us from enthusiastic strangers, drenching us in a powerful atmosphere of happiness, laughter ringing out all along the way.

![Songkran celebration](images/009.jpg)

Phuket has a different local festival every month. After the water fight, everyone was relaxed and chatting warmly. **In that moment, I hardly felt like a traveler anymore. We had lived through this moment together, melted into this place, become close friends. Local Festival Day in Phuket**

**Hour 18 in Phuket**

***The Market Adventure***

"I was born in northern Thailand, where the ingredients are incredibly fresh. Growing up, I followed my family to the local market to buy vegetables, meat, and spices. When I travel to other cities, wandering through the market and experiencing the local 'street life' gives you a much deeper understanding of the place."

![Local market](images/010.jpg)

"Every weekend, Thai locals sell their own homegrown vegetables, flowers, and fruit at these markets. **These ingredients come straight from their own fields — the most local, authentic flavors, with spice blends passed down through generations. The food made from these** is utterly different from what tourists eat at the big food courts set up for them."

**Hour 36 in Phuket**

***Street Food Treasure Hunt***

Mornings in Phuket begin with the chirping of birds and the setting up of street stalls. The moment I opened my eyes, Angie had already sent me a list of her favorite Thai snacks.

![Street food](images/018.jpg)

But since it was still the New Year holiday, many roadside stalls were temporarily closed. We decided to take a ninety-minute flight from Phuket to Bangkok for a wild exploration of the same "street food."

![Bangkok street food](images/019.jpg)

The food stalls hidden in the farmers' markets are like tiny treasures. Walk for ten minutes, and you'll taste at least ten different flavors. Even the same ingredient can easily present multiple dimensions: the crisp freshness of cold dishes, the savory depth of stir-fries, the fragrant crunch of deep-fried bites…

![Miang kam](images/020.jpg)

At a **Miang kam** stall, we took a wild betel leaf, filled it with lime, lemongrass, toasted coconut, ginger, shallots, bird's eye chili, peanuts, and dried shrimp, then added a sweet sauce made from palm sugar and fish sauce. We stuffed the whole thing into our mouths. **"Layer upon layer of texture, like fireworks blooming on the tongue — every chew fills the mouth with fragrance."**

![Grilled skewers](images/021.jpg)

The air was thick with the aroma of charred meat and charcoal: skewers of grilled pork and chicken (Khao Neow Moo/Gai), glazed with sauce, dripping fat slowly onto the coals below.

![Street food](images/022.jpg)

![Street food](images/023.jpg)

A bowl of Thai rice noodles, served with a distinctive chicken-foot broth or a curry fish ball broth. Grab a handful of bean sprouts, green beans, bitter melon slices, and shredded cabbage to go with the soup — sour, spicy, enough to break a sweat.

![Tofu stall](images/024.jpg)

The secret to the delicious sauced tofu at one stall lay in the dipping sauce at the bottom of the bowl. The elderly woman had been making this snack for decades, yet she still treated every customer with the same meticulous care, dipping each piece as if it were her first. "The recipe for her sauce, and her attention to detail, make the taste completely different from every other stall."

Fruit lovers will find deep satisfaction here. A single street has so many tropical fruit stalls, the prices so cheap they're sold by the pile, and the variety is vast.

![Tropical fruits](images/025.jpg)

![Tropical fruits](images/026.jpg)

![Tropical fruits](images/027.jpg)

We'd leave one stall feeling completely full, but at the next, we'd somehow eat just as much again — because it was simply too delicious. There were flavors that fiercely awakened my taste buds with sour, sweet, and numbing heat, and flavors that gently, silently let the tongue discover the beauty of umami.

![Street food](images/028.jpg)

![Street food](images/029.jpg)

![Street food](images/030.jpg)

**Beyond the joy of the taste buds, I felt the sincerity of the locals.** "**I really love Thai people. They have a natural warmth toward others.** Genuine smiles on their faces — none of the indifference you often see in big cities," Angie said.

**Hour 52 in Phuket**

***The Night Market Playground***

![Phuket Old Town](images/031.jpg)

"I love the Old Town. During the day, it's an artsy, cultural neighborhood. On Sunday nights, it transforms into a very cool night market."

![Sunday Walking Street](images/032.jpg)

We had wandered through once during the day, ducking in and out of many old sites. At night, the open spaces between the buildings truly transformed. "Each stall is built by the locals themselves. Some are simple, but they're real. This market has a completely different atmosphere from the tourist-oriented setup at the famous Patong Beach. It's small, but it's really beautiful, really relaxed."

![Night market](images/033.jpg)

![Night market](images/034.jpg)

Lights washed over the buildings like a fairy-tale amusement park. Street musicians played. Even if you're just a lazy foodie today, a simple stroll here lifts the spirits — you'll encounter countless authentic delicacies, performances, and a rich tapestry of local culture.

**Hour 63 in Phuket**

***The Quiet Beach***

"**In Phuket, not every seafood feast is expensive, and not every beach is crowded.**"

Angie loves "From Market to Table" cooking and rejects any form of industrial tourism.

Her Airbnb is in Rawai Beach, the only beach in Phuket where fishing boats dock. "The fishermen bring their freshly caught, live seafood ashore and sell it right there. The more primitive the market, the purer the seafood." It's even better than the seafood feasts at Banzaan or Patong. Pick what you want, and the kitchen prepares it on the spot, quickly serving up a fierce, live lobster.

![Rawai seafood](images/038.jpg)

![Seafood](images/039.jpg)

After the seafood, we went to the beach. "Here, there are no crowds of selfie sticks — it's a private stretch of shore."

Before us: a blue horizon, glittering waves. Someone sunbathing on the sand. Someone singing softly, the song carrying a quiet, contented simplicity.

![Nai Harn Beach](images/040.jpg)

"Normally, we walk a few steps to a beautiful beach that only locals go to. Or we take a boat as a family to a nearly deserted island. I also love riding my Vespa with my daughter to discover places you can't reach by car."

![Quiet beach](images/041.jpg)

**Hour 70 in Phuket**

***Nature's Gift***

![Chalong Bay Rum](images/042.jpg)

"I drive here to drink handmade white rum. You won't find this anywhere else." On Phuket's southeast coast, a French couple extracts raw material from local sugarcane, using a traditional French copper still to produce bottle after bottle of delicious rum.

![Rum distillery](images/043.jpg)

![Rum distillery](images/044.jpg)

The rum carries the sweetness of sugarcane and the fragrance of fresh leaves. Every step of the mixing process is organic, all-natural.

Angie and I are both busy people, but during these three days, we sent each other long messages every day — about her favorite foods, her love for her family. When we sat down to talk, we couldn't stop. **"There's a kind of complete openness and ease."**

![Angie and Yue](images/045.jpg)

"You only live once. I want to eat what I want to eat, play how I want to play, love my family… I've already made a list of things I must do before I die… **Travel is my passion.**" We resonated with each other again and again, recognizing kindred spirits.

She has a sun-soaked laziness and playfulness, but also a brisk, refreshing charm. As a Superhost, she replies to every guest's message with warmth — even though she has an assistant. **"Everyone who comes to Phuket on vacation hopes their holiday will be beautiful, like a dream. I realized my dream, and I want them to live in theirs too."**

**Chalong Bay Rum Distillery**

![Chalong Bay Rum](images/046.jpg)

I remember the early morning my flight landed, the car speeding toward the villa. I didn't know a single person here. I had no idea what stories were about to unfold.

And three days later, parting was so hard. We promised to meet again — at the next stop, and the stop after that.

**Phuket — here, it's high summer every day, a summer that never ends. The food has a charm you can never get enough of. The people are just the right amount of enchanting. 🏠**
