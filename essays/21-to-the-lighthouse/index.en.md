# To the Lighthouse

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

![Lighthouse](images/002.gif)

Today's story is about lighthouses.

The original title was "One Hundred Lighthouses of the World."

We have selected a handful, to take you to those far-flung corners of the earth — hard to reach, harder to leave — to see the lighthouses that stand alone, far from the clamor and bustle of the world.

If there are a hundred lighthouses, and one day you can visit even half of them, you can mark that as having truly journeyed around the world.

![Lighthouse light](images/003.gif)

Everyone carries a lighthouse within their heart. Perhaps it is only in adversity that you can see it clearly.

Just as it is only in the dark of night that you can see the faint glow of a lighthouse.

Today is a day of encouragement. I have written a short letter at the end. But for now, let us talk about lighthouses.

**Stars in the mist, lighthouses in the deep sea — may they warm you through the long winter nights. ▼**

Among Wong Kar-wai's films, I love *Happy Together* most of all.

"I heard there's a lighthouse there. People who've been heartbroken like to go, to leave all the unhappy things behind."

The lighthouse at the end of the world, the Iguazu Falls. If one day I can ride my dreams to the ends of the earth, if one day I stand beneath those falls, I hope there will be two of us standing there.

![Lighthouse from Happy Together](images/004.jpg)

"Go south. To a place called Ushuaia."

"It's so cold there. Why go?"

"I heard it's the end of the world. I really want to see it."

![Ushuaia lighthouse](images/005.jpg)

The lighthouse from *Happy Together* — Faro les Éclaireurs, in Ushuaia, Argentina — stands at the southernmost tip of the South American continent. It is the necessary passage to Antarctica, and the legendary end of the world.

**▼**

![Lighthouse and waves](images/006.gif)

It is the symbol of the soul's light in Virginia Woolf's writing;

It is the crossroads of the mind's nocturnal voyage in *Shutter Island*;

It is the reassuring presence in the hearts of sailors;

It is the **lighthouse**.

It began as a harbor entrance marker, and by the 18th century had gradually evolved into a warning signal for reefs and headlands, becoming a guiding light for those who navigate the seas.

**▼**

![St. John's lighthouse](images/007.jpg)

St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. At the easternmost edge of Canada, keeping watch over the endless Atlantic, year after year.

![Bonifacio lighthouse](images/008.jpg)

Bonifacio, Corsica, France. Napoleon's birthplace, a bright splash of color on the Mediterranean. From here, you can look across and see the statue of Napoleon in the square opposite.

![Boston Harbor lighthouse](images/009.jpg)

Boston Harbor Lighthouse, Little Brewster Island in outer Boston Harbor, Massachusetts. The first lighthouse in the United States, destroyed and rebuilt several times, reconstructed in 1873. It is the only lighthouse in the United States still tended by a keeper.

![Aniva lighthouse](images/010.jpg)

Aniva Lighthouse, Sakhalin, Russia.

Once a place of exile, now uninhabited. This lighthouse, which once held great military significance on the Russia-Japan border, is now regarded as one of the most mysterious abandoned lighthouses in the world.

![Fisgard Lighthouse](images/011.jpg)

Fisgard Lighthouse, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada. The first lighthouse on Canada's west coast, designated a National Historic Site in 1958.

![Peggy's Cove lighthouse](images/012.jpg)

Peggy's Cove, Nova Scotia, Canada. Standing on boulders carved by glaciers along the coastline, it is Canada's most beautiful lighthouse, and the lighthouse featured in the film *Hanamizuki*. There is talk of demolishing it to build a more modern structure.

![Sydney lighthouse](images/013.jpg)

Sydney, Nova Scotia, Canada. Go see the lighthouse on the grasslands. Perhaps one day, nothing will remain but the crumbling base, recording the passage of time.

**"At the end of the pier, the lighthouse's beacon emerged through the morning mist — a small, abandoned lighthouse, as faithful as the one in my memory." — Marc Levy, *The Shadow Thief* ▼**

![Lighthouse in morning mist](images/014.gif)

**The road of life is long and varied, like sailing a distant sea. Sometimes the waters are calm and the journey smooth; other times the waves rage and the passage is hard. But so long as the lighthouse in our hearts never goes dark, we can continue along our course. — Lu Xun, *Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk* ▼**

![Santa Cruz lighthouse](images/015.jpg)

Santa Cruz, California, United States. The California coast, in its overlooked winter, reveals a moving, desolate beauty.

![Iceland lighthouse with aurora](images/016.jpg)

Gerÿar, Gullbringusysla, Iceland. Northern light. A lighthouse set against the aurora borealis.

![Pladda lighthouse](images/017.jpg)

Pladda Lighthouse, Pladda Island, Isle of Arran, Scotland. Scotland is, relatively speaking, one of the more desolate corners of Britain — and it is precisely in this desolation that we can truly appreciate the quiet solitude of a lighthouse.

![Marblehead Lighthouse](images/018.jpg)

Marblehead Lighthouse, Ohio, United States. The oldest lighthouse on the Great Lakes.

![Seltjarnarnes lighthouse](images/019.jpg)

Seltjarnarnes Lighthouse, Gullbringusysla, Iceland. A lighthouse in the aurora — a beauty that feels as though it belongs to another age.

![Lighthouse in sunset](images/020.gif)

**Dreams are like stars — we can never reach them. But they are also like lighthouses — we use them to guide our course. — Hu Xue, *The Gate of the Soul* ▼**

![Bull Island lighthouse](images/021.jpg)

Bull Island, Dublin, Ireland. Rowing toward the end of the world.

![Dakar lighthouse](images/022.jpg)

Dakar, Senegal. This is not only the finish line of the world's most grueling cross-continental rally from Europe to Africa.

![Ōmazaki lighthouse](images/023.jpg)

Ōmazaki Lighthouse, Ōma, Aomori Prefecture, Honshu, Japan. From here, you can watch the sunrise over the Pacific and the sunset over the Sea of Japan.

![Cap Ferret lighthouse](images/024.jpg)

Phare du Cap Ferret, Pyrénées-Atlantiques, Aquitaine, France. Standing 52 meters tall, red and white, it is the perfect place for a 360-degree view of the Atlantic.

![Bellevue Beach lighthouse](images/025.jpg)

Bellevue Beach, Copenhagen, Denmark. An old man, swans, the sea, and a lighthouse — the poetic ease and comfort of a fairy-tale kingdom.

**▼**

Virginia Woolf wrote in *To the Lighthouse*: "She looked out over the boundless blue sea; the grey-white lighthouse stood in the distant haze of smoke and mist; to the right, as far as the eye could see, the green dunes covered in wild grass, gradually crumbling under the lapping of the sea, forming soft, low folds; the silt-laden water seemed to flow endlessly toward an uninhabited fairyland of dreams."

![Burnham-on-Sea lighthouse](images/026.jpg)

Burnham-on-Sea Lighthouse, Somerset, England. The lighthouse on stilts, captured in long-exposure over the water.

![Kampen lighthouse](images/027.jpg)

Kampen Lighthouse, Sylt, Schleswig-Holstein, Germany. This lighthouse stands on the island of Sylt, at Germany's northernmost edge — a white conical stone tower wrapped in a black band, classical and elegant.

![Pigeon Point Lighthouse](images/028.jpg)

Pigeon Point Lighthouse, San Mateo County, California, United States. The tallest lighthouse on the West Coast of the United States.

![Barnegat Lighthouse](images/029.jpg)

Barnegat Lighthouse, New Jersey, United States. First built in 1835, raised four times from its original twelve meters to its current fifty, it is one of New Jersey's most famous lighthouses.

![Cape Reinga lighthouse](images/030.jpg)

Lighthouse at Cape Reinga, Bay of Islands and Northland, New Zealand. Go north, and you reach the end of the North Island — New Zealand's northernmost lighthouse. Here, the Tasman Sea meets the Pacific Ocean. Maori legend also gives this lighthouse a special significance: it guides the spirits of the departed.

![Mull of Galloway lighthouse](images/031.jpg)

Mull of Galloway Lighthouse, Drummore, Scotland. At its southernmost point, open the door, climb the lighthouse, and you can gaze across the Irish Sea to Belfast on the other side.

![Lake Superior lighthouse](images/032.jpg)

Crisp Point Lighthouse, Lake Superior shoreline, Michigan, United States. Perhaps you have seen it, but you have never gazed at it like this.

![Chania lighthouse](images/033.jpg)

Faros, Chania, Crete, Greece. Crete is the birthplace of Greek mythology. The lighthouse was built in 1830, a Venetian-style tower, and is now one of Chania's most iconic landmarks.

![Sant'Elia lighthouse](images/034.jpg)

Faro di Sant'Elia, Cagliari, Sardinia, Italy. A happy homeland forgotten in the western Mediterranean.

![Balearic Islands lighthouse](images/035.jpg)

Far des Cap de Barbària, Balearic Islands, Spain. A family basking in the sunlight beneath the Spanish lighthouse.

![Santa Maria lighthouse](images/036.jpg)

Cabo de Santa Maria, Faro, Portugal. This lighthouse, accompanied by the soundtrack of waves crashing against the shore, radiates a brightness all its own.

![Cape Disappointment lighthouse](images/037.jpg)

Cape Disappointment Lighthouse, near Ilwaco, Washington, United States.

![Bassin lighthouse](images/038.jpg)

Bassin Lighthouse, Bassin, Quebec, Canada. The road to a fairy-tale world.

**A Short Letter to Everyone**

![Letter](images/041.png)

Today is a day of encouragement.

JUSTGO received an invitation from WeChat's official team for the Original Content Protection feature — and it was approved very quickly.

When I opened the back end, I was overjoyed, and also found it hard to believe.

This account is truly the smallest seed I have ever planted in my career in new media. But it is also the only one I have cared for most deeply, the only one I hope will grow and flourish.

I once dreamed of the "Original" label — for someone who creates content, it carries a little sparkle.

But we had only published fewer than ten proper articles. I had thought I would persist until we had over thirty, and then try to apply...

I never expected

...

On this planet, there are over ten million WeChat public accounts, and every day, fifteen thousand more are added.

This feeling — it was as if a faint star in the universe looked up and saw a lighthouse, in the pitch-black night, cast its light upon you.

Then I should catch that light.

Whatever diligence or persistence I have shown in other matters, when it comes to this account, I have been, admittedly, a very capricious editor-in-chief.

We assembled a team of brilliant volunteers, each one handpicked by me from hundreds of emails, each one someone I had spoken with deep into the night.

But because of my travels, my busyness, my choices, my hesitations, this thing and that thing... the update frequency went from daily, to monthly, to once every three months, to not at all...

Last night, I was chatting with Wenyi, the head of our original writing team. Reading her words, I kept smiling, but my heart felt so full.

"We're just waiting for you, waiting for you, still waiting for you. Once you move, we can move forward..."

"I don't want to get too comfortable too early. I feel like if I were in Chengdu, I'd be in such a cozy state that I wouldn't even want to strive. A state of easy contentment — that's not what I want."

"Let us become amazing people. Just thinking about it makes me unable to sleep."

"I'm screenshotting this as evidence. So I don't always feel like you're about to abandon us."

"My classmate asked me recently, why hasn't your account updated in so long? I said, the editor-in-chief is out playing... And you're playing really well!!"

Thank you, to those of you still waiting for me.

Thank you, WeChat, for giving us this encouragement.

I will be worthy of myself, worthy of every trust placed in me. I will be serious about this.

Yue (serious face)
