# The Sweetness of Prague · Love is the Heroic Dream in a Weary Life

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

Love to me,

is not a touch of skin,

not a single meal.

It is an undying desire,

the heroic dream in a weary life.

When I traveled to Prague, this line echoed in my mind.

In this picture-perfect house, I met a pair of artists so deeply in love…

This was my favorite Love Story captured on that journey. True love is kindred spirits, souls in harmony, endless conversation. An inexhaustible tenderness.

![Prague animation](images/002.gif)

**First Glimpse of Prague**

This past summer, I traveled alone through Europe — Paris, Heidelberg, Vienna, Budapest — and at last I laid eyes on the red rooftops of Prague.

![Prague red rooftops](images/003.jpg)

This is the city where Kafka lived his entire life, like an **ancient and beautiful fairy tale**.

I wanted to find a beautiful Airbnb, surrounded by greenery and flowers, with warm and thoughtful hosts, where I could pause and forget the long road.

![Prague Airbnb exterior](images/004.jpg)

I checked into the home of a young artist couple. One is a fashion designer, the other an illustrator. **They are perfect soulmates, their days unfolding like a romantic fairytale.**

![The artist couple](images/005.jpg)

Our story begins in this red-roofed house.

![Red-roofed house](images/006.jpg)

**The Hostess is a Fashion Designer**

![The hostess](images/007.jpg)

Margareta loves making clothes. Since she was fourteen, she has been studying fashion design and art in the Czech Republic.

"**I love beautiful things. I love the details of fabric** — silk, cotton, lace… the texture held in my hand. The moment I touch them, I feel: life is so wonderful." Her eyes sparkled like stars.

![Margareta's workbench](images/008.jpg)

This is her workbench, where she sits for seventeen or eighteen hours every day.

"When you really become an artist or freelancer, you realize it's truly not easy. Making money is always hard. But because of passion, you want to do your absolute best."

![Fashion design](images/009.jpg)

![Garment details](images/010.jpg)

![Clothing pieces](images/011.jpg)

Her garments draw inspiration from nature — every blade of grass, every tree, every fish, insect, bird, and flower. Delicate, refined, airy, full of spirit.

![Design details](images/012.jpg)

![Clothing display](images/013.jpg)

The host is Lukas, an illustrator — a frank, sincere, kind-hearted young man who paints extraordinarily beautiful worlds stroke by stroke.

![Illustrator Lukas](images/014.jpg)

"I drew all of these," he said. I was enchanted by this strange and wondrous world.

**This is the Best Home in Prague**

![The LOFT living room](images/018.jpg)

Pure white, the faint fragrance of plants floating in the air, bright light streaming through the skylight, hand-painted artworks on the walls, open and spacious, minimalist furniture…

I remember the moment I stepped into this loft. Artistry hung in the air. "To us, this is the best home in Prague."

![Inside the LOFT](images/019.jpg)

They prepared a hand-drawn map and a local's guide to Prague for me — these were my most treasured keys to exploring the city.

"Prague is small, but actually it's very big. Beneath the surface, so many interesting things are hidden: small streets, theaters, exhibitions, events, restaurants, and bars. It's fantastic," he said. "**You have to live like a local to taste the magic.**"

![Hand-drawn map](images/020.jpg)

![Prague guide](images/021.jpg)

Walking into my room, it was beautiful as a painting. "This bedroom window — it is also a painting."

![Butterfly room](images/022.jpg)

"And the butterfly is a lovely connection — it links the outdoor garden to within."

"My first English name as a student was Butterfly. What a surprise for me," I said, delighted.

"So this room is yours," he said.

![Room interior](images/023.jpg)

![Room details](images/024.jpg)

The house is full of green plants. "Sunlight, greenery, a cozy sofa to sink into, and a favorite book — it feels like pure happiness."

**Kindred Spirits Always Find Each Other**

![Couple together](images/027.jpg)

I was almost suffocated by their sweetness. "You two are so perfectly matched, like soulmates," I said.

**They met as students, sharing the same passions and temperaments. They are the city's finest freelancers**, growing together, choosing together, advancing and retreating together through life.

![Daily life of the couple](images/028.jpg)

"We met in Finland as exchange students at an art school, both studying design.

We used to live in a small Czech town for a few years. People there stayed in one place all their lives, **never having seen the wider world**, afraid of new things and new people, just watching TV, reading newspapers, cut off from information.

**That wasn't the life we wanted, so we chose to move to a big city.**"

![Streets of Prague](images/029.jpg)

We work on things together, creating independently — illustrations, animations, design — gradually putting down roots in Prague.

Now we have many friends and clients here, we can visit exhibitions anytime, the perfect artistic atmosphere."

![Creative life](images/030.jpg)

![Illustration work](images/031.jpg)

They also have two big dogs, "our children." They walk them together every day.

The dogs were originally strays on the street, dirty and seriously ill, but they insisted on adopting them, nursing them back to health and vigor bit by bit.

**Dinner and Breakfast Together**

We had breakfast together: bread with rich cream, coffee, and stories from travelers all over the world.

"There was a couple in their sixties, staying at a homestay for the first time. They were so worried. When they learned we were entrusting our entire home to them, they couldn't believe it at all. On the day they left, they kept saying thank you, saying how happy they were — and there were actually tears in their eyes."

![Dining together](images/032.gif)

![Breakfast time](images/033.jpg)

Late at night, they rolled up their sleeves and lit the stove to cook dinner just for me. They brought out coconut milk, lentils, African millet, watermelon, and grapes… Watching them washing and chopping, I was deeply moved.

![Cooking dinner](images/034.jpg)

People who love life live with warmth and vigor. In the Czech Republic, a country of meat lovers, they are vegetarians. Their kitchen is fresh and clean, filled with spices brought back from India: sea salt, turmeric, black pepper, cloves, black mustard…

![Kitchen spices](images/035.jpg)

![A corner of the kitchen](images/036.jpg)

**The Best Part of Airbnb is the People**

![A corner of the garden](images/037.jpg)

After hearing their story, I walked into the small garden downstairs. Soft, scattered light filtered through the glass windows. The stone floor was swept perfectly clean. The trees grew lush and full.

![Garden rocking chair](images/038.gif)

I sat down on the rocking chair, drinking a large glass of beer, while a clingy white cat circled around me.

![White cat](images/039.jpg)

After coming in from the garden, I went back to my room and slept for a long time.

![View from the window](images/040.jpg)

I woke once at five in the morning and remembered Murakami's words: "I want to see the sky at dawn gradually turning white, **want to drink steaming hot milk, want to smell the fragrance of morning trees**, **want to turn the pages of the morning paper.**"

The second time I woke was just before sunset. I sat up and gazed at the red rooftops outside the window, the glass revealing a deep blue sky, white clouds drifting far away.

![Red rooftops through the window](images/041.jpg)

**In this home filled with poetry, art, fragrance, and tea, fresh flowers are always blooming on the table, and the people are sweet as honey.**

We talked about Airbnb together, and agreed that the best part is the people.

"People from all over the world, living tens of thousands of kilometers apart, brought together by one house — **finding their kindred in the gentlest, quietest way**. How wondrous."
