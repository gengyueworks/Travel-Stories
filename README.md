# Travel Stories · 旅行故事

Original stories and photographs from years of traveling the world—one place, one person, one moment at a time.
多年环游世界途中写下的原创故事与摄影——一个地方、一个人、一个瞬间，一次一篇。

Every story appears in both Chinese and English. All words and photographs are my own.
每篇故事均以中英双语发布。所有文字与照片均为原创。

Browse the `essays/` directory to read. Each essay contains `index.zh.md`, `index.en.md`, and an `images/` folder.

## About · 关于

I'm Geng Yue, a traveler and writer. For years I have been on the road around the world, and this column is where the road ends up: original long-form stories, told in words and photographs.

Not guides. Not checklists. Stories.

Nothing here is aggregated or reposted. Every photograph was taken by me, and every story happened to me.
