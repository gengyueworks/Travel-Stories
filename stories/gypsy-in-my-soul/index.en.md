# There Is a Gypsy in My Soul — A Stunning Carnival

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

"There is a Gypsy in my soul," and the Seville girl is a Gypsy who wanders to Spain.

### 1 A City of Women and Oranges

When I smell the aroma of oranges,

I have finally arrived in Seville, Spain.

![Seville](images/003.jpg)

The city is filled with gold, sunshine and tropical flair.

The story here never ends,

If you like European literary drama,

These names are from Seville: Carmen, Columbus, Don Quixote, Don Juan, Figaro…

I envy them for their kind of dignified way of life — even if they live very short lives, they must live free.

![Seville streets](images/004.jpg)

"The good-looking skin is the same, the interesting soul picks one."

In fact, the same is true of a city.

I like Seville at first sight.

![April Festival](images/005.jpg)

Every spring, beautiful people gather here.

Everyone puts on good-looking clothes and dresses up

For an ancient festival, the April Festival (Feriade April).

![Blue dress](images/006.jpg)

I wore a blue dress with a big skirt, orange flowers on my head, huge silver earrings, and a red tassel.

I went to the festival with my companions.

### 3 Happy Wanderer

![Wanderer](images/035.jpg)

During my trip to Spain, I read Linda's book and was moved by this sentence:

"The essence of travel is a sense of wandering, especially in exotic cultures that are spread out in different cultures."

"A true traveller will have the elements of the wanderer in his bones, experiencing the happiness, temptation, and adventure of the wanderer." In her soul, there is a Gypsy.

![Gypsy](images/036.jpg)

Gypsies are naturally wandering people. All the way to drifting never stops, from the body to the heart, unconstrained, the caravan is home, and the world is the way of life. (Today, let's not talk about the fact that the Gypsies who have lived in various countries because of historical turmoil have been discriminated against and caught out by the law and poverty, among other things.)

In the past few years, I have been running around on the journey, and every time I arrive in a new place, I fall in love with it. "The distant places that I have never met are my hometown."

![Distant places](images/037.jpg)

Today, we are "wandering" to this city connected to the Gypsies. In this city, there is a song of a Gypsy: time is used to wander, the body is used to fall in love, life is forgotten, and the soul is used to sing.

This is the last stop on our Spanish literary journey. I love these happy people: Potato Sister, Tong Tong, Hehe and Zoe.
