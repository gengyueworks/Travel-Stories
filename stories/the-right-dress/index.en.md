# Wearing the Right Dress Is the Key to Taking Memorable Travel Photos

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

**May we all become "girls on the road" who know how to dress.** May innocence and maturity, handsomeness and sensuality, vintage and modernity coexist on you — and converge to create a stunning version of yourself.

Following up on Vol. 1, in Vol. 2 I'll walk you through **the preparation process before departure, the principles of matching — as practical as it gets.**

Before each trip, preparing clothes is **the most time-consuming yet most enjoyable part.** Everyone can create a brand-new, blooming version of themselves — according to their own personality and preferences, combined with the local landscape.

### Greece · The Inspiration

**Keyword: my favorite blue 💙  carefully prepared**

Greece has used up the best **blue** in the world.

This was the first time I consciously prepared clothes for a trip. **Because it was Greece** — I had finally arrived in the land of my dreams.

![The blue-and-white world of Santorini](images/002.jpg)

![Blue-domed church in Santorini](images/003.jpg)

**How to Prepare**

Timing: the week before departure.

Here's my preparation process, step by step:

-

Open the **itinerary** for Greece and pull out the names and **keywords** for each destination — Day 1, Day 2, and so on.

-

Plug the keywords into **Google Images or Maps** and you'll see the island or city directly.

-

After seeing the photos, I choose the **corresponding colors** and match the clothes.

-

Roughly figure out how many days and how many looks I need, then start searching and filtering on my go-to shopping sites and apps.

(This process is actually a bit tedious on the eyes, and you'll need to communicate with sellers to confirm sizes and delivery times. I suggest starting as early as possible.)

![Greek island scenery](images/004.jpg)

![Blue-and-white architecture](images/005.jpg)

On the first day, I stayed in the town of Oia, in the northern part of Santorini. Oia is a gathering place for many artists — houses like thick, white snow. At that moment, I had truly walked into a blue-and-white world, like a fairy tale.

Here, blue-and-white clothes are the perfect match for the blue sea and white houses.

![Blue dress, candid shot on the stairs](images/006.jpg)

![Blue dress against a blue door](images/007.jpg)

During a lunch break on Mykonos, my friend 🍑 captured the first photo of me walking down the stairs in this blue dress.

The upper body is a layer of translucent blue tulle; the blue of the skirt below perfectly mirrors the blue door.

At that time, my identity was that of a travel photographer. I styled the outfits purely on instinct — I just wanted to "do justice to the beauty of my dreams." **The travel companion on that trip was truly the perfect partner and confidant.** I'm grateful for the candid photos she took — even years later, I still love them deeply.

![Full shot of the blue dress](images/008.jpg)

Tip: A fitted waist with a voluminous lower half — the high-waist golden ratio makes you look taller. A long skirt naturally carries an elegant, dignified temperament.

![Blue-and-white striped dress detail](images/010.jpg)

This striped mini dress is the freshest blue-and-white combo for summer. Unlike the long dress above, the short dress reflects a youthful, lively temperament.

**The vertical stripes** visually slim the figure. **The deep ocean vibe** is perfect for summer.

**For accessories**, I also added a **large royal blue bag** to enhance the overall color. This bag was actually one I used to carry when I worked in Beijing — I never expected it to be so perfect for a trip to Greece.

Add a **straw hat**, and the holiday look is complete. Simple, sweet, with a touch of freshness — that's what this look is meant to convey.

### Kenya, Africa · The Inspiration

**Keyword: denim jacket + off-shoulder tie-dye dress — old clothes, new combinations**

![The Maasai Mara grasslands, Kenya](images/013.jpg)

**The freedom and wildness of Kenya** — countless people have listed it as a must-visit destination in this lifetime.

![Sunrise over Mount Kilimanjaro](images/014.jpg)

Everyone wants to go to Africa, a place they've never set foot in, with a desire to explore the unknown. In those few days, **I saw the breathtaking sunrise over the snow-capped peak of Kilimanjaro, the dazzling starry sky, and the minimalist beauty of the Maasai Mara** — pure blue sky, vast rolling grasslands, and solitary acacia trees standing alone.

This time I didn't buy new clothes. Instead, I **paired together some pieces that had been sitting idle in my closet for a long time.**

![Bohemian print dress layered with a denim jacket](images/015.jpg)

I have a long Bohemian print dress that, worn on its own, carries a slightly nomadic, wandering spirit.

But on this trip to Kenya, I was a little tired of the same old floral holiday dresses. I wanted something different. So I **mixed in a washed-denim short jacket** that I'd bought years ago and rarely had the chance to wear. Put together, these two pieces brought out **a handsome, wild flavor.**

![Pink blossoms in an African garden](images/016.jpg)

And on that afternoon, my impression of Africa was completely overturned. Leaves blotted out the sky, and pink blossoms bloomed wildly. I spun around and thought of Sanmao's words: *The wind whispers through the trees, the birds are calling, and the flowers have fallen in my dreams, I know not how many.*

My advice: take some **old clothes** you've already bought, and based on the destination, create a permutation, a collision. There'll be a surprise. Maybe the clothes you normally keep at the bottom of the box turn out to be incredibly photogenic. **Be bold, try anything — there are no taboos.** As long as it looks good.

My favorite travel photo from that European trip is of me wearing **this backless white dress in the rose garden of Empress Sissi.**

It would also be wonderful for a beach holiday, but spinning around in a European garden, it has a dreamlike feeling. The fluttering hem captures every smile, every glance — preserved in the memory of Vienna's casual, flowing atmosphere.

### Everything, in the End, Is a Matter of the Heart ❤️

I once saved a passage: *"There are many beautiful things in the world, and on the road, some are presented in material form — clothing, architecture, scenery, food, objects. Some are presented in spirit — artistic creation, philosophy, civilization, tradition, faith…*

*Some are grand, like the pyramids; some are small, like walking into a café in the afternoon, ordering a coffee, and sitting quietly in the sun.*

*All forms of manifestation eventually come down to our hearts* ❤️.

*When the heart is clear, everything will reveal its beauty. Otherwise, we don't notice it, we can't feel it.*

*First, observe and cultivate your own heart. Strive to do all the small things in life, and do them well. When we've stabilized our physical and mental strength, then let's try setting out for faraway places."*

**Wearing clothes in your favorite colors** — that, in itself, is already a practice of inner independence.

**It is trust in and confirmation of your true self. Recognize yourself, believe in yourself — that is beauty.**
