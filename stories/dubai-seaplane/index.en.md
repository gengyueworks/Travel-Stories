# We Chartered a Seaplane to See Dubai

*Geng Yue · Travel Stories*

✈️

Flying high above Dubai, my left hand recorded video while my right hand took photos — I couldn't rest for a moment. I simply wanted the people I care about to see the beauty that stunned me, and to feel the heartbeat at the same frequency in that moment.

In 2016 I flew 150,000 kilometres and visited many cities around the world.

"Flight" was the keyword of my year. My friends even joked I was an "air hostess". My feelings about flying can be summed up in a favourite quote from the artist Leonardo da Vinci:

> "Once you have tasted flight, you will forever walk the earth with your eyes turned skyward, for there you have been, and there you will always long to return."
> — attributed to Leonardo da Vinci

It was another three hundred years after he wrote those words before the first aeroplane was invented.

My last journey of the year was Dubai — winter is its best travel season, and it had drawn me for a long time: the strangest, richest buildings in the world, and that unique landscape of half azure Arabian sea, half golden desert. To view Dubai from a bird's-eye perspective, in the air, was a way of travel I very much looked forward to. Just thinking about it made me a little excited.

One morning we boarded a seaplane from Seawings. The small white aircraft seats nine people. With four cool photographer friends, we experienced a chartered flight.

As the plane began to glide across the water's surface, my adrenaline surged — that familiar thrill of leaving the ground.

Our captain was handsome, if a little aloof. As the white plane climbed higher and higher, he even raised his iPhone to photograph the scenery...

I soared through the blue sky, looking down like a bird at this city of wonders.

The weather that day was perfect — bright, dazzling sunshine, and the stunning scenery spread out in a panorama of blue sea and sky. The Palm Jumeirah, the eighth wonder of the world, floated on the sea like a mirage, dotted with the world's top luxury hotels, villas and water parks. The Burj Khalifa, the tallest human structure — 828 metres, the highest point human creation can reach — gathered brilliant light in the sun, as if a miracle. The skyscrapers glittered with gold, like exquisite works of art.

It was an ultimate experience. During the 25-minute flight, we admired the views, moving from one window to another to shoot frantically. Thrilling, and yet left us wanting more.

If you travel to Dubai someday, I recommend you "fly once" — look down on the city of wonders like a bird. In those moments in the clouds, you feel as if you've left your ordinary life behind.

For me, the world can sometimes feel tiring and mundane, but travel is my cure.

Perhaps 99% of our lives revolve around the same fixed routines. But allow yourself 1% of your time to let go, leave your desk, even leave the earth's surface — and take a happy flight.

## About the Seaplane

Some information that might help you:

**Q: What can you see from the sky?**

The core of the tour is Dubai's two iconic towers — Burj Khalifa and Burj Al Arab. Flying through the blue sky, you'll see all of Dubai's famous landmarks, including the world's most luxurious hotel Burj Al Arab, the world's tallest building Burj Khalifa, and the eighth wonder of the world — the famous Palm Jumeirah and The World islands.

**Q: How long is the flight, and can you fly with companions?**

The flight lasts 25 minutes. Each plane has nine guest seats.

**Q: How do you book?**

You can book via website, phone or email. The website is [www.seawings.ae](https://www.seawings.ae).
